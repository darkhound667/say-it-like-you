# Brief ideate-a: product vision round — learning science and delight

## Goal
Write `IDEAS-A.md`: 8 bold, specific ideas to make "Say it like you" a product people would actually keep using. IDEAS ONLY, no code changes.

## Context
Read `API_CONTRACT.md`, `server.py`, `frontend/index.html`, `IDEAS.md` (an earlier round: do not repeat it; go further). Product: visitor clones their own voice with Sarvam, then hears new words and phrases in 12 Indian languages in THEIR voice, can record an attempt that is word-diffed against the target via STT. Think like a language-learning product designer: what does hearing yourself speak correctly unlock for retention, motivation, accent work and confidence? Consider spaced repetition, minimal pairs, retroflex/aspirated sounds, script reading, code-mixed phrases, travel and family-conversation use cases, parents teaching kids, diaspora learners, accessibility (dyslexia, speech therapy), gamification.

## Files you may touch
- IDEAS-A.md (only)

## Requirements
For each idea: name, pitch, the 10-second wow, who it is for, effort (S/M/L), exact Sarvam APIs/routes needed (existing or new with JSON shape), risks. End with a ranked top 3 and a one-paragraph product narrative for a hackathon judge pitch. Under 120 lines.
